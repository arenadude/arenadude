What the Dude does

Everything below is free, for everyone.

Rates every card

A plate under each card on the draft screen: its HearthArena tier score and its Firestone winrate. Golden and signature cards too.

Picks for you

Both sources combined into one pick, said out loud by the mascot. With commentary.

New

Scouts the opponent

At the mulligan, the 10 cards your opponent's class loves most in Arena, so you know what to play around.

Fixes your redraft

After a loss, which cards to cut from the deck, from the worst up.

Watches your run

Your record per run, a verdict on the finished deck, and a reaction to every win and loss.

Install in 2 minutes

  1. Download and move to Applications

    Get Arena.Dude.Mac.zip, unzip it and drag Arena Dude into your Applications folder.

  2. Open it, then click Done

    Arena Dude isn't notarized by Apple yet, so macOS stops it the first time. That's expected.

  3. Privacy & Security → Open Anyway

    Open System Settings → Privacy & Security, scroll down to "Arena Dude" was blocked to protect your Mac and click Open Anyway.

  4. Open Anyway again, and confirm

    Confirm with Touch ID or your password. You do steps 2–4 only once.

  5. Let the Dude see your screen

    When macOS asks, click Open System Settings, turn on Arena Dude in Screen & System Audio Recording, then quit and reopen it. Without it the mascot can't see your draft.

  6. Go to the Arena, click Allow

    Start Hearthstone and head to the Arena. macOS then shows a privacy reminder over the game ("… requesting to bypass the system private window picker"). Click Allow: the Dude can't read what the dialog covers, and after Allow macOS shows it less often. It may come back from time to time; the mascot will tell you. On the first run the Dude also downloads the card images, about a minute.

FAQ

Is it free?

Yes. Arena Dude is free and open source, and every feature that helps you win is for everyone. Patrons on Patreon get the animated Dude as a thank-you.

I'm a patron. How do I unlock the animated Dude?

Your code is in the patrons-only post on Patreon. Right-click the Dude, choose Unlock animated mascot… and paste it.

How does it know what's on screen?

It reads Hearthstone's log files and looks at the screen to read the cards you're offered, like other deck trackers. It never touches the game client or its memory.

Why does macOS say it can't verify the app?

Apple only skips that warning for apps notarized through its paid developer program, which Arena Dude isn't part of yet. The install steps above get you past it once.

Windows? Other languages?

Not yet. Arena Dude runs on Macs with Apple Silicon and reads card names in English, so Hearthstone has to be set to English.

Where do the ratings come from?

Tier scores from HearthArena, winrates and the opponents' top cards from Firestone, card data from HearthstoneJSON. They're refreshed twice a month.

Found a bug?

Right-click the Dude and choose Report a problem: it opens #bug-reports on our Discord and shows your log file, ready to attach. You can also open an issue on GitHub.
